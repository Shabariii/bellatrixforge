The Forge

Bellatrix is the warrior star on Orion's shoulder, the blue giant that names our first game and our studio.

We are a small independent studio from Brazil. We make games with a voice of their own: a pilot and her guardian under a dying star, a troll and a skull arguing in a swamp hut.

Every game is hand-directed, tested by bots and by people, and shipped only when it feels right.
