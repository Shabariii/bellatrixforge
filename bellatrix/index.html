The Star Is Changing

Bellatrix, the blue giant of Orion, is in transition. Something in the asteroid belt is corrupting the change, and it has turned our own fleet against us.

You launch alone. ECHO, the memory of the ancients, speaks in your ear through every climb.

BELLATRIX is a survivors-like space shooter: move, choose your upgrades and survive the swarm, then face the fortress that closes every level.

Survive the Swarm

Every level-up offers a hull module, a passive or a skill. Flak that airbursts, lightning that chains, plasma that burns. Each card shows the numbers before and after.

Fortress Bosses

THE BASTION, THE ARMORY, AEGIS REVENANT. Named fortresses with phases and telegraphed laser sweeps that force you to move.

Light the Starforge

Between runs, stardust lights the stars of a growing constellation, unlocking weapons, passives and skills for the next launch.

A Pilot's Ceremony

Register your callsign, feel the dock ignite, warp into the sector and return to Mission Control between flights.
