Three verbs

How it plays

Move

Your fighter has real momentum. Drift, turn, and cut through the swarm with an offensive dash. Your hull weapons do the shooting; your hands do the surviving.

Choose

Every level-up offers a hull module, a passive or a skill. Each card shows the numbers before and after, so you always know what your build is doing.

Break the Fortress

No boss, no clear. Every level ends against a named fortress with phases and telegraphed laser sweeps. Move, survive, then hit the exposed core.

What waits in the hangar

The Wake

Splash and Chain

Flak that airbursts, lightning that jumps from hull to hull, plasma that burns. Weapons are modules mounted on your ship, and they evolve as you level.

Named Fortresses

THE BASTION, THE ARMORY, AEGIS REVENANT. Our own stations and our own flagship, still wearing our crest.

The Starforge

Between runs, stardust lights the stars of a growing constellation, unlocking weapons, passives and skills for the next launch.

ECHO

Your guardian intelligence and the memory of the ancients. She talks you through the campaign, one line at a time, and never cuts herself off.

Mission Control

Station, campaign map, hangar, Starforge and records. Register your callsign, feel the dock ignite, and warp into the sector.

Your Way to Play

Keyboard or gamepad. EASY and NORMAL in the demo. Built to run in a browser on an ordinary laptop.
