02

How it plays

You work. He brews.
1

Type

Every key you press, in any program, stirs the cauldron. The game counts the rhythm, never what you write.

Type like rain, not like hail.
2

Heat it

In a steady rhythm the pot climbs from SIMMER to ANCESTRAL: sparks, coloured smoke, fireworks across your whole screen.

The pot spat. Say thank you. Nobody will.
3

Sell and survive

Type like a maniac and it blows. Vosk goes down face first. Selling potions pays the skull's rent.

You look half dead. I respect that.
03

What is on the shelf

Hung up by the skull
Voice

A skull with opinions

Old Teeth keeps the books, takes the coins and has a dry, rude line for every mood.

Rhythm & risk

Find your brewing rhythm

Six stages of boil. Sparks, smoke and fireworks. Push too hard and the pot boils over. Vosk gets up. He is used to it.

Customers & craft

Strange brews. Stranger company.

Over 40 kinds of visitor and dozens of recipes. Collect ingredients, brew gross potions and let Vosk regret the taste test.

Your desktop companion

A little hut. A long way to heal.

From bandages to a full shaman. Gear up, wake the blue runes and keep brewing above your taskbar. Transparent, click-through, hidden during fullscreen apps.

04

Life in the hut

Game art, untouched

Who knocks

Visitors walk, hop, fly and ride in. Scroll sideways.

Potion shelf

A Thunder Burp, a Witch Special, a Night Drop. Each potion has a line and a star rarity.

Ingredient shelf

Nothing arrives by itself: you collect, choose, mix. Few items in hand, each one counts.

Outside

The hut from outside, with a door that opens and a sign that lies.

The blue runes

Twelve carved posts wake one by one after Vosk becomes Rune-Marked. Each gives a small bonus.

07

What the skull can see

Old Teeth has no eyes, and neither does the game's memory. It notices that you are typing and how fast. Never what. Nothing leaves your PC. Quit any time from the tray icon.

The file is not code-signed yet. Windows may warn you the first time: More info, Run anyway.
