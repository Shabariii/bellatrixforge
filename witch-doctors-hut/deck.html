How it plays

You work. He brews.

1. Type

2. Heat it

3. Sell, or blow it

Every key you press, in any program, stirs the cauldron. The game counts the rhythm, never what you write.

Steady rhythm climbs six stages: SIMMER, BUBBLING, ROILING, WITCHFIRE, STORMBREW, ANCESTRAL. Sparks, smoke, fireworks over your whole screen.

Type like a maniac and the pot boils over: BOOM, Vosk goes down face first. Sold potions pay the skull's rent.

The troll and the skull

Two voices, one hut

Vosk. Blue swamp troll back from a war. Half a tusk, one good eye, a hole in his belly. He lands on a flaming cannonball in front of his uncle's hut. The sign says GONE ON VACATION. BACK SOMEDAY. MAYBE.

Old Teeth. The family's founding ancestor, now a skull with a candle on his head and opinions about everything. He keeps the books and takes the coins.

"Who let a half-dead troll in my hut?"
"Fine. Not a trespasser. A lodger. Lodgers pay rent, boy."

Where it runs

What exists, and what is only a plan
In development

Windows

The main game. A transparent window above the taskbar, counting keys in any program. Portable exe built, about 280 MB. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, no account, no internet.

  • Click-through, draggable
  • Hides under fullscreen apps
  • Quit from the tray
Testing

Phone, in the browser

Tap to stir. A different product from the taskbar: short sessions, no keyboard. Treated as an experiment, with no store release promised.

  • Web build exists
  • App stores: not started
  • Mic features need HTTPS
Planned

Full-screen hut

A free update after launch: the whole hut in a big window, a door to new rooms. Not built yet. Keeps the taskbar game as the product and gives a second launch moment.

  • Free update, no date
  • Same save
  • Sandbox first

The studio

Bellatrix Forge Studios

One person, one star. Bruno builds and decides everything that enters the game. An AI-assisted pipeline does the heavy lifting: art, voices, balance bots and QA scripts, all declared openly.

  • Art made with AI tools, then cut, aligned and edited by hand
  • Voices and some music generated, English voice only
  • Every economy change is played by bots before it ships
  • Pixel and material rules written down in STANDARDS.md
  • Bellatrix is a blue giant star in Orion: the studio's home sky
